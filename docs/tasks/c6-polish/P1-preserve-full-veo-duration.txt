OWNER-APPROVED DURATION SEMANTICS

The current implementation estimates a short presenter duration from word count and then uses that estimate as an exact FFmpeg `trim`/`atrim` boundary. Live evidence showed a seven-second finished clip cutting off speech from the provider's eight-second Veo result.

Remove that behavior.

- The word-count duration remains useful only for presenter-copy planning and deciding whether the current Veo path needs the initial 8-second generation only or the existing extension path.
- Once Veo returns presenter media, the final simple headline must preserve the full returned Veo timeline.
- Do not use the estimated/planned speech duration as an FFmpeg trim boundary.
- Do not discard the final second of an 8-second Veo result because copy was estimated at 4-7 seconds.
- Do not discard retained extension coverage from a 15-second Veo result because copy was estimated shorter than 15 seconds.
- If deterministic normalization needs a duration bound for audio/video synchronization, derive it from the qualified raw Veo media/provider coverage, never from the word-count speech estimate, and do not shorten the usable Veo timeline.
- Preserve all available presenter speech and video through the end of the raw Veo clip.

This deliberately supersedes the prior rule that `--max-seconds` is a hard final-media ceiling. Keep the CLI option and 4-20 validation, but treat it as the copy/planning ceiling used to bound presenter text and provider extension selection. The final media may reflect Veo's supported 8- or 15-second coverage even when that is longer than the speech estimate or requested planning ceiling. Update only the narrow current docs/help/comments needed so they no longer promise a final trim that the owner has removed.
